# Workflow 03 — Teach and Resume

## Trigger
“Continue my curriculum”, “Explain Scope N”, or a learner asks a follow-up during a lesson.

1. Read the saved progress, learner concept state, current curriculum, approved guide and latest reviewer notes.
2. If a user requests a specific feature, honor that request. Check prerequisites and briefly establish only the missing foundations needed; do not force them through already-understood units.
3. If the next guide is missing or stale, run the guide/review workflow before presenting it as approved. A preliminary explanation may be offered if useful, but clearly label it preliminary.
4. Start with a meaningful question/outcome and why the feature matters. Continue from what the learner already knows. Do not repeat the whole roadmap every turn.
5. Present a coherent learning portion of suitable depth. Choose example, visual, source trace, worked demonstration or brief prediction based on the concept and learner signals. There is no fixed size.
6. Notice feedback. If the learner asks what a term means or says they are lost, stop advancing and identify the missing concept. Explain with a different representation or more basic prerequisite.
7. If the learner demonstrates understanding, continue without needlessly repeating the same explanation. A small real application or prediction can be a useful check when appropriate.
8. Answer questions directly; after resolving them, reconnect to the curriculum where relevant.
9. Update progress based on evidence: `exposed` means presented; `developing` means partial understanding; `understood` requires meaningful evidence; `needs_revisit` means confusion remains. Do not award understanding solely because the learner said “yes”.
10. Persist the current unit, lesson position, concepts known/confused, questions, review status and next step.

## Conversational rules
- Avoid forced “say next” endings. Offer a useful natural continuation only when it helps.
- Do not make every interaction a quiz.
- Do not dump the entire codebase or all stages in one turn when the learner asked to learn it progressively.
- If the learner explicitly asks for a comprehensive standalone guide, provide one, structured from foundations to detailed reference.
